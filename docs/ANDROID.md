# Android app

Let Me Wonder for Android is a small app that opens Instagram's own website,
with the filter built in. It isn't in the Play Store: you install it once from a
link, and fixes then arrive by themselves.

## Install
1. Open the install link Claude gives you on your **Android phone**. It's a page on
   expo.dev, the free service that builds the app. Tap **Install** (or download the
   `.apk` file).
2. Android asks whether your browser may install apps ("install unknown apps").
   Allow it **for this once**; you can turn it off again afterwards in the same
   setting.
3. If Google Play Protect says the app is unrecognised, choose **Install anyway**.
   It says this about every app that doesn't come from the Play Store.
4. Open **Let Me Wonder** and log in to Instagram. You log in on Instagram's own
   page; the app never reads your password.
5. Go through the [checklist](CHECKLIST.md).

You can now uninstall the official Instagram app if you want to.

## Using it
- **Back:** the Android back gesture goes back inside Instagram. From the home
  feed it leaves the app.
- **Settings:** the small round button at the bottom right (it hides itself in
  DMs and stories). It has one switch per part, all on by default, and they apply
  immediately.
- **Links to other websites** open in your normal browser.
- **Photos and videos:** sending photos in DMs and posting work through the usual
  picker. The app asks for the camera or microphone only if you use them.

## Something slipped through?
Stay on that screen, open settings → **Copy problem report**, then paste it to
Claude with a word on what you saw. The report holds the page's *structure*:
usernames are replaced with `{user}`, captions and DM messages are left out, and
anything you've typed is never included.

## Updates
When Claude fixes something, the app downloads the fix in the background and uses
it the next time you open it. To get it right away: settings → **Check for
updates**. You never need to reinstall. Even when a new install is needed, it
installs over the old one and you stay logged in.

## Privacy
The app shows Instagram's website and keeps Instagram's login cookie on your
phone, like a browser does. It sends nothing anywhere and stores only your switch
settings.
