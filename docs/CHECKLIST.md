# Checklist: try these on your phone

Go through it once after installing, and again when something seems off.
If an item fails, open the filter settings → **Copy problem report** on that
screen and paste it to Claude, saying which item failed.

## Should be gone
- [ ] **Reels tab:** the bottom bar has no Reels button.
- [ ] **Reels in the feed:** scroll the home feed; no reels or video posts.
      (This hides reels from people you follow too. If you'd rather see
      friends' reels, turn off "Reels in the home feed".)
- [ ] **Ads:** scroll about 30 posts; nothing marked *Sponsored*.
- [ ] **Suggestions:** no "Suggested for you" carousels or suggested posts in
      the feed. After **"You're all caught up"**, nothing more loads.
- [ ] **Explore:** the Search button shows the search box, but no grid of
      photos and videos.
- [ ] **Hashtags:** searching a hashtag and opening it shows the **Blocked**
      screen.
- [ ] **Reels page:** opening `instagram.com/reels/` shows the **Blocked**
      screen, and **Go back** works.
- [ ] **Profiles:** no Reels tab and no "Suggested for you" row.
- [ ] **Reel from a friend:** a reel sent to you in DMs plays. Swiping to the
      next one, or tapping another reel from there, shows the **Blocked**
      screen; **Go back** returns to the reel.

## Should still work
- [ ] **DMs:** read, reply, send a photo.
- [ ] **Stories:** watch friends' stories.
- [ ] **Search:** find an account by name and open their profile.
- [ ] **Your profile** opens normally.
- [ ] **Posting:** the **+** button lets you post a photo.
- [ ] **Notifications:** the heart shows your notifications.
- [ ] **Stay logged in:** close Safari (or the app) completely, reopen
      Instagram, and you're still logged in.

## iPhone only
- [ ] **Home Screen icon:** the full-screen test in [IPHONE.md](IPHONE.md),
      step 5. Tell Claude whether the Reels button was gone.
