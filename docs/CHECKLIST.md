# Checklist: try these on your phone

Go through it once after installing, and again when something seems off.
If an item fails, open the filter settings → **Copy problem report** on that
screen and paste it to Claude, saying which item failed.

## Home feed
- [ ] **Home:** the app opens on Instagram's own home page, with the stories
      row at the top, and the Home button keeps you there.
- [ ] **Keeps loading:** scroll down; new posts keep coming until you've seen
      them all, without getting stuck on the loading spinner, and without the
      same posts showing up again further down.
- [ ] **No jumping back:** scrolling down, the feed never jumps back up to a
      post you already passed.
- [ ] **Friends' reels:** reels from people you follow appear in the feed;
      suggested reels don't. (To hide every reel, turn on "Reels in the home
      feed": each one then becomes an empty card, the same size, with a small
      grey "Reel hidden" on it. To try small boxes instead, turn on "Small boxes
      for hidden posts"; if the feed starts jumping again, turn it off.)

## Should be gone
- [ ] **Reels tab:** the bottom bar has no Reels button.
- [ ] **Ads:** scroll about 30 posts; nothing marked *Sponsored*.
- [ ] **Ads in stories:** watch several stories in a row; an ad (*Sponsorizzato*)
      disappears by itself within a moment, and the story after it plays. Then tap
      the left side to go back: you pass the ad again and land on the story before
      it. If an ad stays, tap the settings button on the right edge and copy a
      problem report.
- [ ] **Locked filters:** in the settings, App (problem report, updates,
      reload) and Display work without the code; the Filters switches are grey
      until you enter it; a wrong code says so; after closing and reopening
      they're locked again.
- [ ] **Suggestions:** no "Suggested for you" / "Consigli per te" carousels or
      suggested posts in the feed. After **"You're all caught up"**, nothing
      more loads.
- [ ] **Explore:** the Search button shows the search box, but no grid of
      photos and videos.
- [ ] **Hashtags:** searching a hashtag and opening it shows the **Blocked**
      screen.
- [ ] **Reels page:** opening `instagram.com/reels/` shows the **Blocked**
      screen, and **Go back** works.
- [ ] **Profiles:** no Reels tab and no "Suggested for you" row.
- [ ] **Reel from a friend:** a reel sent to you in DMs plays. Swiping to the
      next one, or tapping another reel from there, shows the **Blocked**
      screen; **Go back** returns to the reel.

## Should still work
- [ ] **DMs:** read, reply, send a photo.
- [ ] **Stories:** tap one in the row at the top of Home. Watch it, close it,
      and you're back where you were in the feed; open another one.
- [ ] **Search:** find an account by name and open their profile.
- [ ] **Your profile** opens normally.
- [ ] **Posting:** the **+** button lets you post a photo.
- [ ] **Notifications:** the heart shows your notifications (only the
      "Suggeriti per te" accounts at the bottom are hidden).
- [ ] **Stay logged in:** close Safari (or the app) completely, reopen
      Instagram, and you're still logged in.
